# MingoQuiz v1.0.0

**Status: draft.** Finished in stage E of the [release plan](../release-plan.md); the date and the final list of known issues go in then.

The first release of MingoQuiz, the ISC Racing Team's training site for the Formula Student registration quizzes. Everything is new: there is no previous release to upgrade from.

## What's in it

- **Accounts:** invite links, passwords (Argon2id), roles (member, reviewer, admin) and team positions, admin tools for invites, positions, alumni and reset links ([ADR 0002](../adr/0002-invite-and-password-auth.md)).
- **Question bank:** every FS-Quiz quiz mirrored politely and imported with its answer keys, images, and that year's rulebook and handbook beside each question ([fsquiz-api](../fsquiz-api.md)).
- **Training:** practice by topic, a timed daily question per area with streaks, replays of real past quizzes against their original clock ([players' guide](../guides/players.md)).
- **Scoring:** a rank with LP that goes up and down, an account level from XP that only goes up, season leaderboards per person, area and vertical, with opt-out ([game rules](../game-rules.md), [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-ranked-lp-and-account-level.md)).
- **Live quizzes** for team meetings: a host, tables per sub-department with captains, routing by topic, a projector view, a CSV of the results ([hosts' guide](../guides/live-quiz-hosts.md), [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-live-quiz.md)).
- **Review tools** for the question bank: reported, changed upstream, unclassified, not graded and hidden queues ([reviewers' guide](../guides/reviewers.md)).
- **Personal data:** privacy notice, JSON export and real deletion by the member, retention jobs ([ADR 0006](../adr/0006-personal-data.md)).
- **Operations:** hardened Docker Compose stack, deploy with automatic roll back, nightly and pre-deploy dumps, a restore that survives a dropped session ([runbook](../runbook.md)).

Two functional red-team sweeps tested it before release; their findings and fixes are in [remediation](../redteam/remediation.md).

## Known issues

Accepted for this release (sweep 2, see the [release plan](../release-plan.md#4-what-blocks-the-release)); they get fixed in later releases:

- Scoring details waiting on the Technical Directors: a blind pick pays on the four single-choice questions with two right options (S2-DOM-01); the hint's "half" doesn't match the real stakes (S2-DOM-02); the typed-answer hint floor (S2-DOM-03); undoing a mistaken lowering of position moves LP (S2-ACC-02).
- Live quizzes: tables sharing a topic don't take turns across sessions (S2-LIVE-01); projector figures stretched and options below the fold on some screens (S2-UI-02); the offline banner covers the countdown (S2-UI-01).
- The reviewers' guide quotes a message the app never shows (S2-UI-05).
- A damaged database can't be restored without a safety dump (S2-OPS-03).
- Low and Info findings of both sweeps.

## After deploying

First deploy of an environment: follow [runbook 1.5](../runbook.md#15-first-deploy-of-an-environment) (create the first admin, then `deploy/refresh-bank.sh <env>`), with the Nginx snippet `deploy/nginx/quiz.conf` installed and Nginx reloaded.
