FS-Quiz (fs-quiz.eu, by Yannik Ottens) collects every published registration quiz. Its public API needs no key. The data is ODbL: we attribute it and, if we ever publish our labelled version, we publish it under ODbL too.

The quiz decides if we race at FSG

What the FSG quiz looks like
  • 9–12 questions per quiz
  • 6–10 minutes per question, 1–2 hours in total
  • Rules, engineering maths and scoring, all mixed
  • Ranked against every other team: a few points decide the slots
Where we stand today
  • We cram the week before instead of training all season
  • Nobody knows who in the team covers which topic
  • 79 % of past questions have no worked solution
  • No record of how we did or what we got wrong

Not all of these are needed. The cheapest high-value pair is the daily question plus the weekly mock quiz. The solution bounty doubles as study material and as something we can contribute back to FS-Quiz.

12 Content B
University Microsoft tenants often require admin approval for third-party apps; the magic-link fallback removes that dependency. Notion sync is optional and can come after the pilot.

Three ways to build it

A · Repo only
  • Mock quizzes from the command line
  • No accounts, no leaderboard, no cost
B · Chat + Notion
  • A bot posts the daily question in the team chat
  • Answers logged in a Notion database
C · Web app
  • Static site, Supabase for sign-in and data (free tier)
  • Daily question, mock quiz, leaderboard
Recommendation
  • Pilot C for four weeks with only the daily question and leaderboard
  • Keep A as the offline fallback
9
15 Content C
B is the fastest to launch but hard to extend (mock quizzes, images, timers). C costs a few weeks of work from one or two people and scales to everything on the ideas slide.
